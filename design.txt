Design Document - SmartCart AI E-Commerce Platform
1. Design Vision

The design aesthetic is:

Modern
Minimal
Premium-looking
Product-focused
Clean and spacious

The UI heavily emphasizes:

Large product visuals
Rounded cards
Bright accent colors
Soft shadows
Minimal text clutter
Smooth shopping experience

This style works extremely well for:

Tech accessories
Gadgets
Electronics
AI-assisted shopping platforms

Basically: “Apple-inspired minimalism meets colorful startup landing page.” The internet discovered rounded corners in 2020 and collectively decided sharp edges were emotionally hostile.

Typography Hierarchy
Element	Style
Hero Heading	Extra Bold
Product Titles	Medium/Bold
Descriptions	Light/Regular
Buttons	Semi-bold
Key Observation

The design avoids long paragraphs and keeps text extremely short.

This is smart because users don’t read product descriptions. Humanity evolved language and then collectively decided “wireless headphone good” is enough information.

8. Animation Recommendations
Use Subtle Animations
Card hover scaling
Smooth page transitions
Floating hero image animation
Button hover glow

Avoid:

Excessive motion
Complex loaders
Heavy animations

Otherwise your e-commerce site starts behaving like a Marvel movie trailer.

9. Mobile Responsiveness
